# Habits + Trackers visual review — 6 October 2026

Screenshots show the actual native debug app under the separate `com.dom.os.nativeverify` identity, using synthetic UUID sample profiles only. They are not mockups or installed-stable screenshots. Quick Check-In is captured within the Trackers overview (06 and 07 intentionally show the same surface). The skipped state is visible in Habits and History; the paused state has its own capture.

Spacing and hierarchy: consistent padded headers, grouped actions and two-column detail cards follow the refined Goals language. Primary capture/log actions stand out; advanced links and destructive actions are tucked behind disclosure/confirmation. No overlapping or clipped controls were observed in the reviewed desktop captures. Long logs scroll normally.

Density and readability: short Habit capture takes two main fields; Tracker capture offers a template plus name/type with advanced preferences collapsed. Today, flexible period progress and all records have distinct headings. Neutral tracker values retain readable contrast. Secondary information is quieter; native window sizes constrain the number of cards visible before scrolling.

Dark premium direction: restrained green surfaces, fine borders, clear titles and quiet status language are consistent with Goals refinement. No wall of red, punitive missed-day display, gamified streaks or fake metrics. Existing Dashboard presentation is intentionally preserved.

Empty states and truthful progress: fresh/sparse histories explain that a chart needs two recorded days. A quota can exceed its target (6/4 shown) without fabricated daily failures. Newly started quotas currently show 0/0 completed historical periods; truthful but a future prose empty-state could read more naturally. Minimum met is distinct from target met. Paused/skipped states keep their history without implying failure.

Mobile-conscious layout: scoped flexible grids collapse at narrow widths, controls have usable targets, keyboard focus is visible and Reduce Motion is respected. These screenshots are desktop observations, not a complete mobile-device or accessibility certification.

Fixes from review: page-edge spacing, min-content overflow protection, end-exclusive period wording, raw boolean log values, singular opportunity/entry wording, non-due Habit wording, archive gaps, stale Assistant read results and partial-completion evidence. All final checks pass. Some unaffected captures precede the last wording-only build; affected detail/History/Assistant captures were refreshed.
